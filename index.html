3-WEEK SPRINT

The entire HPL Season 1 unfolds over 3 weeks of structured innovation sprints.

COMPETITION

3 Tracks. 3 Weeks. 1 Champion.

Each track features one overarching problem statement divided into 3 progressive phases. You pick a track and commit to all three phases.

01
TRACK A

TechForGood

Build solutions that address real civic, social, or environmental challenges in the Udupi region. Create tech that matters.

Phase 1: Problem & Ideation
Phase 2: Prototype
Phase 3: Full Build
02
TRACK B

AI / ML Challenge

Harness the power of artificial intelligence and machine learning to solve complex, data-driven problems of tomorrow.

Phase 1: Data & Research
Phase 2: Model Training
Phase 3: Deployment
03
TRACK C

Open Innovation

Complete creative freedom. Build anything — web, mobile, hardware, or a system — that pushes the boundaries of what's possible.

Phase 1: Concept & Design
Phase 2: Core Features
Phase 3: Polished Product
